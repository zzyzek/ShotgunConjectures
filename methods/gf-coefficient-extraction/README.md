# Generating-function coefficient extraction

**When it applies.** Both sides are coefficients of power series or polynomials with rational or algebraic
generating functions. An alternative to creative telescoping that needs no certificate search.

**Method.**
- Write both sides as one coefficient of the same polynomial, expanded two ways (`add_pow`); e.g. A005430:
  2n·[x^(n−1)] (1+x)(1+x+x²)^(n−1) with 1+x+x² = (1+x)² − x and = x + (1+x²).
- Read a sequence from its e.g.f. in ℚ⟦X⟧ with `PowerSeries.coeff_mul` and antidiagonal sums (A025191).
- For a transform, treat substitution (`PowerSeries.subst`) as a ring map, check one polynomial identity
  modulo the algebraic relation of the inner series (`linear_combination` with a quotient found by sympy),
  then cancel a nonzero factor in the domain ℚ⟦X⟧ (A108863).

**Pitfall.** Coefficients of noncomputable power series cannot be checked by `decide`; compare with the OEIS
terms in Python and state that in the README.

**Worked example.** [OEIS/A005430](../../OEIS/A005430/README.md); also OEIS/A025191, OEIS/A108863.
