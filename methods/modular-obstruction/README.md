# Modular obstruction

**When it applies.** "X = Y has no solutions beyond ...", "every member of a family is a sum of ...".

**Method.** Compute the residue sets of both sides modulo small m (polynomial variables over Z/m, exponentials over
their eventually periodic orbit). Disjoint residue sets mean no solution. For homogeneous equations add
descent and restrict to primitive tuples.

**Certificate.** The modulus and the two residue sets; a `decide` check over Z/m.

**Pitfalls.**

- Exponential residues are eventually periodic, not periodic from 0: handle the pre-period.

**Code.** `methods/oplib/modular.py`

**Worked example.** [NT/F23.2](../../NT/F23.2/README.md), Lean [NT/F23.2/F23_2.lean](../../NT/F23.2/F23_2.lean)
