# Ordered prime gap bound for exact multiplicative targets

**Method.** List the prime factors in increasing order. Every proper part A of n must sit at least one unit away from the target, which forces the next prime below k(A + 1) via (A + 1)(p - 1)^k < A p^k and a Bernoulli-type inequality. Induction over the ordered primes gives an explicit bound in terms of K, hence finiteness for each K (Pomerance 1977 for Lehmer's problem). Works when n is squarefree or exponents are otherwise controlled; fails when one prime sets the target itself or exponents are unbounded (A123365 with three or more factors).

**Worked example.** [FAM/lehmer-totient](../../FAM/lehmer-totient/README.md)
