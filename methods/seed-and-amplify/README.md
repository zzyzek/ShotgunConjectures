# Finite seed plus composition or tensor-power amplification

**Origin.** Drafted 2026-11-05 from the OpenAI mathematics manuscript collection (github.com/openai/math, snapshot 2026-10-26), families OAI 130, OAI 132, OAI 192, OAI 099, OAI 284. Paraphrased from abstracts and skimmed proofs; not yet used on a problem in this repo. Confidence that this is one technique: high for 130, 132, 192; medium for 099 and 284

**Method.** Find one small object whose two measures already violate the target inequality by a constant factor (a Boolean function with bs(f,0) > s(f)^2, a gadget retaining more variance than its degree cost, a matrix whose k-th tensor power needs fewer than k m^(k-1) applications). A composition lemma shows the measures behave multiplicatively (or additively in logs), so iterating turns the constant-factor gap into a power gap or an unbounded ratio. A finished argument has the seed (checked exactly), the composition lemma, and the exponent computation.

**Fit to small problems.** yes: seeds are small exhaustive-search targets (Boolean functions on few variables, small matrices); the composition lemma is often elementary. Fits OEIS-style extremal ratios for Boolean functions and codes.

**Lean examples (OpenAI repo).** `lean/OAI/Combinatorics/Sensitivity`, `lean/OAI/Combinatorics/BooleanFunctions`, `lean/OAI/Computability/FourierCircuit`, `lean/OAI/Computability/FourierTransform`
