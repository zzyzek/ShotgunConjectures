# Units, Pell equations, continued fractions

**When it applies.** Sequences of n with a quadratic form value square ± constant; recurrences with palindromic coefficients; a(k+1) = a(k)^2 − 2.

**Method.** Identify the number field and fundamental unit; solution classes are orbits under the unit, which gives
the recurrence and its exact range of validity.

**Certificate.** The field, the unit, the orbit decomposition, and the initial terms.

**Pitfalls.**

- Several orbits can interleave; a recurrence fitted to the first terms may fail when a new orbit starts.

**Worked example.** [OEIS/A182334](../../OEIS/A182334/README.md)
