# Cofactor exponent for Wieferich-type congruences

**Method.** For c = p^k m with p prime, the order of a mod p^(2k) divides c - 1 and the group exponent p^(2k-1)(p - 1); it is coprime to p, so it divides p - 1 and hence m - 1. So p^(2k) | a^(m-1) - 1 for every prime-power part. With a certified factorisation of a^(m-1) - 1 (Pratt certificates checked in Lean) this excludes whole families of c with one unknown prime.
