# Finite verification of identities

**When it applies.** OEIS "Conjecture: a(n) = formula", "g.f. is ...", "satisfies the recurrence ...".

**Method.** Put both sides in a class with a size bound; agreement on enough terms proves equality. C-finite of
orders d1, d2: d1 + d2 terms. Quasi-polynomial of degree d and period P: (d + 1)P terms. Polynomial identity
of degree ≤ d in a parameter: d + 1 values. P-recursive: guess the recurrence, prove each side satisfies it,
compare initial terms.

**Certificate.** The class and its bound, the proof that each side lies in the class, the term comparison.

**Pitfalls.**

- The step "each side satisfies its recurrence" needs a proof (closure properties, transfer matrix); say how.

**Code.** `methods/oplib/seqtools.py`

**Worked example.** [OEIS/A182334](../../OEIS/A182334/README.md), Lean [OEIS/A008676/A008676.lean](../../OEIS/A008676/A008676.lean)
