# Parametric families plus a finite check

**When it applies.** "Every n ≥ N0 has a representation / two representations ...".

**Method.** Find polynomial families covering all residues (or all large n) with the required property, often by
fitting to computed small representations; then check exhaustively below the threshold of the families.

**Certificate.** The families with the identity they satisfy, the residue coverage, and the finite table.

**Pitfalls.**

- Check the side conditions of the families (distinctness, ordering, positivity) on the whole range, not just the identity.

**Worked example.** [NT/F19.1](../../NT/F19.1/README.md), Lean [NT/F19.1/F19_1.lean](../../NT/F19.1/F19_1.lean)
