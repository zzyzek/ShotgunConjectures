# Apply a classical theorem

**When it applies.** Coman-type "infinitely many pseudoprimes of form ..." claims and similar, where a classical construction already gives the family.

**Method.** Recognise the claim as an instance of a known theorem or construction (Cipolla's pseudoprimes,
Chernick's Carmichael family, Dirichlet's theorem, Zsigmondy, Fermat's little theorem) and check the
hypotheses.

**Certificate.** The theorem with citation and the verification of its hypotheses.

**Pitfalls.**

- Such items are usually "solved in principle"; say so and check whether the source already knew it.

**Worked example.** [COM/C003](../../COM/C003/README.md)
