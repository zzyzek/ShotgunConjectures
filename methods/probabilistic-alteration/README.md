# Random construction with alteration and union bounds

**Origin.** Drafted 2026-11-05 from the OpenAI mathematics manuscript collection (github.com/openai/math, snapshot 2026-10-26), families OAI 025, OAI 084, OAI 112, OAI 132, OAI 157, OAI 160, OAI 161, OAI 191, OAI 196, OAI 329. Paraphrased from abstracts and skimmed proofs; not yet used on a problem in this repo. Confidence that this is one technique: medium (umbrella)

**Method.** Sample from a structured law (random routing on a tree, random partitions, random labels over finite fields), show each bad local event is rare and their dependence is controlled, delete or patch the few bad parts. The gain over classical results usually comes from the structured law, not the deletion.

**Fit to small problems.** yes: unit-fraction (025) and covering/avoidance (084) constructions are Erdos-problem scale.

**Lean examples (OpenAI repo).** `lean/OAI/MeasureTheory/DyadicAvoidance`, `lean/OAI/Geometry/HeilbronnTriangle`, `lean/OAI/Algebra/GroupRing`
