# Analytic reduction to a compact region plus rigorous computation

**Origin.** Drafted 2026-11-05 from the OpenAI mathematics manuscript collection (github.com/openai/math, snapshot 2026-10-26), families OAI 005, OAI 090, OAI 189, OAI 229, OAI 266, OAI 119. Paraphrased from abstracts and skimmed proofs; not yet used on a problem in this repo. Confidence that this is one technique: high (explicit in the PDFs read)

**Method.** Prove the statement by theory outside a compact parameter box or outside finitely many cases, then close the remainder with an exact certificate: interval arithmetic and Bernstein coefficients on a grid (229), rational sum-of-squares or moment certificates (266), interval-verified LP auxiliary functions (090), nonvanishing of finitely many fixed matrices mod p (005), or thousands of enumerated cases (189). A finished argument states the reduction precisely, publishes the certificate data and a checker.

**Fit to small problems.** yes: same as our exact-arithmetic and lean-certificate methods, extended to real-parameter inequalities (Bernstein/interval grids); useful for OEIS asymptotic or inequality conjectures with a finite leftover range.

**Lean examples (OpenAI repo).** `lean/OAI/NumberTheory/Catalan`, `lean/OAI/Analysis/PlanarPacking`, `lean/OAI/Combinatorics/Ramsey`, `lean/OAI/Analysis/MutuallyUnbiased`
