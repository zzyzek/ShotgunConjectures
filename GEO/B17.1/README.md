# CFG B17: smallest dimension of a simplicial polytope with non-unimodal f-vector is 20

**Source.** H. T. Croft, K. J. Falconer, R. K. Guy, *Unsolved Problems in Geometry*, Springer 1991, section B17. Björner and Lee found simplicial polytopes with non-unimodal
f-vectors in dimensions 24 and 20; the book asks for the smallest dimension in which
unimodality fails, both for simplicial polytopes and in general (Björner: true for simplicial
d ≤ 15).

**Result.** Simplicial case: 20. Eckhoff showed that simplicial d-polytopes have unimodal
f-vectors for d ≤ 19, and the 20-dimensional examples of Björner and of Lee are non-unimodal.
Literature answer. The general (non-simplicial) case asked in the same sentence is not settled
by this record.

**Argument.** None beyond the citations.

**Evidence.** Web search 2026-11-01: Major & Tóth (arXiv 1501.00430) and an FPSAC 2023 paper
state that Eckhoff proved unimodality for simplicial d ≤ 19 and that 20 is the least dimension
with simplicial counterexamples. Eckhoff's paper itself was not retrieved
(J. Eckhoff, Combinatorial properties of f-vectors of convex polytopes, Normat 54 (2006),
title from memory). No Lean.
Tier 3 literature check 2026-11-05: G. M. Ziegler, *Convex polytopes: extremal constructions and f-vector
shapes* (arXiv:math/0411400), Theorem 2.10, states the simplicial answer (unimodal for d ≤ 19, fails for
d ≥ 20).

**Review level.** `agent`. Partial: general polytopes not addressed.
**Prior art.** As above. 2026-11-01.
