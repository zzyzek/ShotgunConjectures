Subject: Settled conjectures in 230 OEIS entries (list and proofs)

Dear OEIS editors,

The attached list covers 230 OEIS entries whose comments state a conjecture
that is now settled: 202 true, 17 false, 11 trivial or misstated as
written. For 19 of them the answer is already in the literature and the
entry only needs a cross-reference; for the other 211 no source we found
states the full answer
(entry, cross-referenced entries, edit history, web, arXiv; checked
2026-11-04).

The results come from an AI-assisted scan (Claude agents) of open problems.
Each was re-derived by a second agent, and 206 are proved in full in
Lean 4 (core Lean or Mathlib), checked by the Lean kernel; 6 are partly
formalised (the remaining step is named) and 18 have written proofs
only. Every proof, its Lean file and the scripts are in

  https://github.com/zzyzek/ShotgunConjectures

one directory per problem. Where a conjecture needed an interpretation (an
index shift, a reading of the definition), the problem README says which.

Files:
  outreach/OEIS-LIST.md       one line per entry
  outreach/oeis-updates.jsonl one JSON object per entry: anum, kind,
